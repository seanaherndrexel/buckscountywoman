Tell us what we're missing

The best story ideas come from readers. If you know about a new women-run business, a clinic that changed its hours, a support group that deserves attention or a problem nobody is covering, we want to hear about it. You can use the form below or go to our submit page, which also takes events, meetups and local finds.

If your tip is sensitive, say so in your message. We won't publish your name without your permission.

If we got something wrong

Please tell us. Send the headline or link, what's wrong and, if you can, where we can confirm the right information. We check every correction request, fix errors quickly and note the change on the article. Phone numbers and addresses on our resources page get priority, because someone may be relying on them. Our full process is in the editorial standards.

Other reasons to write

  • To add your business to the directory, go to Get Listed.
  • To advertise or sponsor, go to Advertise.
  • To ask about our 10% giving pledge, go to Giving.
  • To report an accessibility barrier, go to Accessibility.

We're a magazine and can't give emergency help. If you are in danger, call 911. Our Resources & Safe Spaces page lists crisis lines that answer around the clock.
