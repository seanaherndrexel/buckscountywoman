One dollar a month, and you're in

Membership to Bucks County Woman costs $1 a month. We set the price that low on purpose, because we want every woman in the county to be able to join.

What your dollar covers

  • A full digital issue every Thursday. You get every article in each issue, readable on your phone, tablet or computer.
  • The whole site. Members can read everything we publish, including the full archive.
  • The members-only community app. You'll join a private space where Bucks County women meet by town and by interest, share recommendations and find events. Read more on the app page.

Pay once for the year

If you'd rather not see a charge every month, you can pay $12 for the full year. It's the same membership at the same price, billed once. Card processing takes a much smaller bite out of one $12 payment than out of twelve $1 payments, so the annual plan sends more of your money to the magazine and to our giving pledge.

Add the print edition

The print edition of Bucks County Woman is an optional add-on for members who want the magazine delivered to their mailbox. It's priced to cover printing and mailing, and we plan to offer it at $5 a month on top of your membership.

The print add-on opens with our first print issue in Spring 2027. Until then, free copies will be available at pickup spots around the county once that issue is out. The print edition page has the details.

Where ten cents of every dollar goes

We give 10% of all proceeds to local organizations that serve women in Bucks County, and membership dues are part of that. We're confirming our partner organizations now and will name them once they've agreed. We'll also publish the amount we give each quarter on our giving page.

Questions members ask

Can I cancel whenever I want?

Yes. You can cancel at any time and you'll keep your access through the end of the period you've already paid for. Monthly members aren't locked into anything.

Do I have to live in Bucks County?

The magazine and the community are built for women who live or work in Bucks County, and everything we publish is local. If you've just moved here or you work here and live nearby, you're welcome too.

What can I read without a membership?

Our Resources & Safe Spaces page is free for everyone and always will be. The business directory, public event listings, selected articles and the weekly email are free as well. Membership opens the full magazine and the community app.

How is my payment handled?

Payments will be processed by Stripe, a payment company used by millions of businesses. We never see or store your full card number. Our privacy policy explains what we do keep.

Who is welcome in the community app?

Every woman is welcome, including trans women, and members must be 18 or older. The app has clear community guidelines that keep it kind and keep politics and religion debates out.
