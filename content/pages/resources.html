If you are in danger, call 911.

This page is free for everyone and always will be. Every phone number and web address below was checked against the organization's own website on October 1, 2026. Hours and services change, so please confirm when you call. If you spot something out of date, tell us and we'll fix it right away.

If someone checks your phone or computer, consider using a device they can't access, and clear your browser history after visiting this page.

When you need someone right now

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Call or text 988 at any hour of any day. It's free and confidential. Spanish speakers can press 2 or text AYUDA to 988. You can also chat online at 988lifeline.org, where deaf and hard of hearing callers will find a videophone option.

Bucks County Mobile Crisis (Lenape Valley Foundation)

Call 1-877-435-7709. A crisis worker will talk with you by phone and can send a team to you if it's needed. The service is open to Bucks County residents of all ages, Monday through Friday from 9:30 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. and weekends from 9 a.m. to 9:30 p.m. Outside those hours, call 988. More at lenapevf.org.

PA 211

Dial 211 or text your ZIP code to 898-211 to reach a specialist who can point you to local help with food, rent, utility bills and other needs. If 211 doesn't connect from your phone, call 1-855-567-5341. You can search on your own at southeast.pa211.org.

Domestic violence

A Woman's Place

The hotline is 1-800-220-8116, and it answers 24 hours a day. It's free, private and confidential, and it's open to anyone age 14 and older, including friends and family who are worried about someone. A Woman's Place is Bucks County's domestic violence organization, and the hotline is the way into all of its services. More at awomansplace.org.

National Domestic Violence Hotline

Call 1-800-799-7233 at any hour, or text START to 88788. Live chat is available at thehotline.org. Deaf and hard of hearing callers can reach the Deaf Hotline by videophone at 855-812-1001.

Sexual assault and other crimes

Network of Victim Assistance (NOVA)

The 24-hour hotline is 1-800-675-6900. NOVA gives free and confidential support to victims of sexual assault and other crimes in Bucks County, including crisis help, counseling and advocacy through the court process. More at novabucks.org.

RAINN National Sexual Assault Hotline

Call 1-800-656-4673 at any hour, or text HOPE to 64673. Support is confidential and available in English and Spanish. Online chat is at rainn.org.

Housing

Bucks County Housing Link

Call 1-800-810-4434 if you're homeless or about to lose your housing. This is the county's single starting point for shelter, prevention programs and housing help. Staff answer Monday through Friday from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. and Saturday from 8:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m., and you can leave a voicemail at any time. The first call takes about 15 minutes. More at fsabc.org.

YWCA Bucks County housing programs

YWCA Bucks County runs several housing programs, including rapid rehousing and a temporary shelter for women and children leaving human trafficking. Entry is through the Housing Link number above. The main office is at 2425 Trevose Road in Trevose, (215) 953-7793. More at ywcabucks.org.

Food

Bucks County Opportunity Council

The Opportunity Council leads the county's food network, which includes food pantries, community meals, senior food boxes and free produce markets. Find a pantry near you on its food pantries map, or read about the food program. Its offices can also help with housing and utility assistance:

  • Upper Bucks (Quakertown): 215-536-0353
  • Central Bucks (Doylestown): 267-733-0529
  • Lower Bucks (Bristol): 215-781-2661
  • Main office (Doylestown): 215-345-8175

Legal help

Legal Aid of Southeastern Pennsylvania

The helpline is 877-429-5994, open Monday through Friday from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. You can apply online at any hour at lasp.org/apply. Legal Aid gives free civil legal help to people who qualify by income, including Protection from Abuse orders, custody, evictions and other housing problems, and public benefits. It has offices in Doylestown and Bristol, and interpreters are available. More at lasp.org.

LGBTQ+ support

PFLAG Bucks County

PFLAG Bucks County is a local volunteer group for LGBTQ+ people, their families and allies. It holds a monthly in-person meeting in Doylestown and a monthly virtual meeting, and it keeps a list of local affirming resources. Email buckscountypflag@gmail.com or visit pflagbuckscounty.org.

Bucks LGBTQ Center

This is a private counseling practice offering LGBTQIA-affirming therapy, with offices in Buckingham and Newtown. Call (215) 260-7570 or visit buckslgbtq.com. Ask about fees and insurance when you call.

LGBT National Hotline

Call 1-888-843-4564 for confidential peer support. Hours are Monday through Friday from 2 p.m. to 11 p.m. and Saturday from noon to 5 p.m., Eastern time. A separate line for older adults, the LGBT National Senior Hotline, is 1-888-234-7243. More at lgbthotline.org.

Trans Lifeline

Call (877) 565-8860 to talk with a trans or nonbinary peer operator. The line is open Monday through Friday from 1 p.m. to 9 p.m. Eastern time. More at translifeline.org.

Disability services

Bucks County Center for Independent Living

The center serves people with all types of disabilities across the county. It offers advocacy, information and referral, independent living skills, peer support and help with transitions. It's at 3466 Progress Drive, Suite 111, in Bensalem. Call 215-781-5070, email info@buckscil.org or visit buckscil.org.

Older women and caregivers

Bucks County Area Agency on Aging

Call 267-880-5700, Monday through Friday from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. The agency helps older adults and their caregivers with in-home support, home-delivered meals, Medicare counseling, senior centers and transportation. The office is at 55 E. Court Street, 3rd Floor, in Doylestown. More at buckscounty.gov.

Elder abuse hotline

Call 1-800-243-3767 at any hour to report abuse, neglect or exploitation of an older adult in Bucks County. The line is run by the Area Agency on Aging.

Pregnancy and postpartum

National Maternal Mental Health Hotline

Call or text 1-833-852-6262 at any hour. Counselors offer free, confidential support in English and Spanish to anyone who is pregnant or has recently given birth. More at mchb.hrsa.gov.

Postpartum Support International HelpLine

Call 1-800-944-4773, or text "Help" to the same number. For Spanish, press 1 when you call or text 971-203-7773. Volunteers connect you with local providers and support groups, and calls and texts are answered between 8 a.m. and 11 p.m. Eastern time. This is not a crisis line. In an emergency, call 911 or 988. More at postpartum.net.

How this list is kept

We include a service only after confirming its details on the organization's own website, and we leave out anything we can't confirm. We recheck the list regularly and after any reader report. Listing a service here isn't an endorsement, and none of these organizations paid to appear. Bucks County Woman can't provide emergency help, medical care or legal advice.
