Federal agencies posted at least ten recalls in the past six weeks that reach Pennsylvania kitchens, including ground cinnamon pulled for elevated lead and a store-brand applesauce pulled for a mold toxin. This page tracks those notices, shows who owns the grocery chains operating in Bucks County, and lists local farms and markets you can buy from directly. Every item links to the agency notice or to the farm's own website.

Recalls and alerts on our radar

Last checked October 1, 2026. Recalls change daily. Each entry below repeats the reason given in the agency notice and nothing more. Open the linked notice for lot codes, dates and refund instructions before you throw anything away.

Lead and heavy metals

  • Lior Cinnamon Ground Seasoning, 90g (3.2 oz), September 24, 2026. Galil Importing Corp recalled UPC 794711005484, lot GAP11304, because the product "has the potential to be contaminated with elevated levels of lead," a finding that came from Maryland Department of Health testing. It was distributed to stores in Pennsylvania, New York, New Jersey, Texas, Florida and Illinois between November 18, 2025 and September 7, 2026. No illnesses were reported. FDA notice
  • Lontcs Busy Board toys, September 10, 2026. SQT recalled about 1,835 boards sold on Amazon from May 2024 through July 2026. The Consumer Product Safety Commission says the zipper "contains levels of lead that exceed the standard set in the federal lead paint ban." No incidents were reported. CPSC notice
  • Imported aluminum and brass cookware (standing FDA advisory). The FDA warns that certain imported cookware "may leach significant levels of lead into food" and keeps a running list of the products it has tested. The agency last added items on December 3, 2025. FDA advisory
  • Ground cinnamon (standing FDA alert). The FDA maintains a table of ground cinnamon products it found with elevated lead and advises consumers to stop using and discard them. FDA alert

Chemical and toxin contamination

  • Wholesome Pantry Organic Unsweetened Apple Sauce, 23 oz, announced September 14, 2026 and posted by the FDA on September 22. Wakefern Food Corp. recalled UPC 04119005677 with a best-by date of June 16, 2027 for elevated levels of patulin, a toxin produced by mold on damaged apples. It was sold at ShopRite, Price Rite Marketplace, The Fresh Grocer, Gourmet Garage and Fairway Market stores in nine states including Pennsylvania. No illnesses were reported. FDA notice
  • Niwali Tejocote Capsules and Tejocote Pieces, September 21, 2026. Global Mix Inc. recalled the supplements, sold online, after the FDA determined the products appear to be yellow oleander, a plant that is toxic to people and animals. No illnesses were reported. FDA notice
  • Lipofit Extreme 2.0 Fat Burner dietary supplement, August 31, 2026. Ana Salazar Modela Tu Cuerpo Inc. issued a nationwide recall for undeclared fluoxetine and 2,4-dinitrophenol (DNP). FDA notice

Bacteria, foreign material and inspection problems

  • Bettergoods Authentic Italian Lemon Alfredo Fettuccine, frozen, 22 oz, October 1, 2026. Gias Foods, Inc. recalled UPC 194346442706, sold at Walmart stores nationwide, because of possible Listeria monocytogenes contamination. No illnesses were reported. FDA notice
  • Martina brand mangoes, PLU 4584, August 28, 2026. Panorama Produce recalled mangoes sold at select Walmart stores in Pennsylvania, New Jersey, New York and Connecticut from August 10 to 21 for potential Salmonella contamination. No illnesses were reported. FDA notice
  • Raw pork, beef and goat products from Star Meat Delivery Inc., September 22, 2026. The USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service announced a recall of roughly 167,639 pounds of meat produced without the benefit of federal inspection and shipped nationwide. No confirmed illnesses were reported. USDA FSIS notice
  • Expert Grill wire bristle grill brushes, October 1, 2026. Walmart recalled about 4.4 million brushes sold from January 2021 through August 2026. The CPSC says bristles "can detach from the brushes and stick to the grill or food, posing an ingestion hazard." No incidents were reported. CPSC notice
  • Fromm canned dog food (Turkey Pâté and Diner Classic Milo's Meatloaf), August 21, 2026. Fromm Family Foods recalled the two products for potential metal contamination. FDA notice

Five lookups worth bookmarking

You don't have to wait for a news story. These official pages are updated as notices are issued, and most offer free email alerts.

  • FDA Recalls, Market Withdrawals and Safety Alerts covers most packaged food, produce, supplements, medicines, cosmetics and pet food. The page has an email subscription option for new recall notices.
  • USDA FSIS Recalls and Public Health Alerts covers meat, poultry and egg products. FSIS offers email subscriptions for recall notices.
  • CPSC Recalls and Product Safety Warnings covers toys, cookware, kitchen tools and other household goods. The CPSC sends recall emails to subscribers and takes consumer reports at SaferProducts.gov.
  • Pennsylvania Department of Agriculture posts state warnings, including raw milk advisories, in its newsroom.
  • FoodSafety.gov Recalls and Outbreaks combines FDA and USDA food recalls in one feed.

If you have a recalled item, follow the notice. Most direct you to stop using the product and return it for a refund or discard it. If you think someone has been exposed to lead, the FDA's advice is to talk to your healthcare provider.

The names behind the store signs

Most of the supermarkets in Bucks County belong to national or international parent companies. A few are family owned or cooperatively owned. Here's the ownership picture as the companies themselves describe it.

  • Giant: The GIANT Company, based in Carlisle, Pennsylvania, is a brand of Ahold Delhaize USA, the American arm of Netherlands-based Ahold Delhaize. Ahold Delhaize
  • Acme: Acme Markets is a banner of Albertsons Companies, based in Boise, Idaho. Albertsons terminated its merger agreement with Kroger in December 2024 and remains a separate company. Albertsons Companies
  • ShopRite: ShopRite stores are owned by the member families of Wakefern Food Corp., a retailer-owned cooperative based in New Jersey. Wakefern
  • Wegmans: Wegmans Food Markets is a family-owned company based in Rochester, New York. Wegmans
  • Whole Foods Market: Amazon has owned Whole Foods Market since 2017. Whole Foods Market
  • Trader Joe's: Trader Joe's is privately held and has been owned since 1979 by the family behind Germany's Aldi Nord. Background
  • Aldi: Aldi stores in the United States are operated by Germany's Aldi Süd group, a separate company from Aldi Nord. Aldi US
  • Lidl: Lidl US is part of Germany's Schwarz Group. Lidl US
  • McCaffrey's Food Markets: McCaffrey's is family owned, with Bucks County stores in Yardley, Newtown, New Hope and Doylestown. McCaffrey's

Recent changes in Bucks County

  • March 2026: Weis Markets closed its store at Mercer Square Shopping Center, 73 Old Dublin Pike in Doylestown, on March 7. The space is slated to become a 51,000-square-foot McCaffrey's, with an opening planned for early 2027, according to Patch.
  • September 2026: McCaffrey's acquired Pennington Quality Market in New Jersey, bringing the family-owned company to nine stores, BUCKSCO.Today reported.
  • 2026: Albertsons closed or marked for closure at least 12 stores nationwide, including two Acme stores in New Jersey. No Pennsylvania stores appeared on the list published by Fast Company in May.

Local farm stands and markets

Buying from a farm doesn't replace reading recall notices, but it does shorten the chain between the field and your kitchen, and you can ask the grower how the food was raised. The details below come from each farm's or market's own website. Hours shift with the season and the weather, so check the link before you drive.

Upper Bucks

  • Trauger's Farm Market, Kintnersville. Route 611 and Island Road, Kintnersville, PA 18930. A 60-acre, family owned and operated fruit and vegetable farm with an on-farm store open year-round. Fall hours: Monday to Friday 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., Saturday and Sunday 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. traugersfarm.com
  • Bolton's Farm Market, Silverdale. 1005 E. Main Street, Silverdale, PA 18962. Sells turkey, chicken and beef raised on the family farm, which the farm describes as antibiotic- and hormone-free. Market hours: Monday to Friday 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., Saturday 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., closed Sunday. boltonfarmmarket.com
  • Blooming Glen Farm, Perkasie. 98 Moyer Road, Perkasie, PA. The farm states it is 100% certified organic and grows vegetables, fruits, flowers and herbs on 40 acres. It sells through a CSA and at two seasonal farmers markets. bloomingglenfarm.com
  • Hershberger Heritage Farm, Sellersville. 1614 Ridge Road, Sellersville, PA 18960. Grass-fed beef, pasture-raised heritage pork, pasture-raised chicken and eggs. Store hours: Monday and Wednesday 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., Friday and Saturday 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., Sunday 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., closed Tuesday and Thursday. bergerbox.farm
  • Quakertown Farmers Market, Quakertown. 201 Station Road, Quakertown, PA 18951. An indoor market with more than 100 departments selling local produce, meats and baked goods. Friday and Saturday 9 a.m. to 9 p.m., Sunday 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. quakertownfarmersmkt.com
  • Perkasie Farmers Market, Perkasie. Run by the borough every Saturday, 9 a.m. to noon, rain or shine, through October 31. perkasieborough.org

Central Bucks

  • None Such Farm Market, Buckingham. 4458 York Road, Buckingham, PA 18912. Fresh produce, beef and other meats, prepared foods and house-baked goods. The farm describes itself as a "100% NON-GMO Grower." Tuesday to Saturday 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., closed Sunday and Monday. nonesuchfarm.com
  • Solebury Orchards, New Hope. 3325 Creamery Road, New Hope, PA 18938. Apples, peaches, blueberries and other fruit, plus applesauce and apple cider, with pick-your-own in summer and fall. Open Thursday to Sunday 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. through October, then Thursday to Sunday 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. in November and December. soleburyorchards.com
  • Manoff Market Gardens and Cidery, New Hope. 3157 Comfort Road, New Hope, PA 18938. A farm market and cidery open year-round. Wednesday to Saturday 10 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., Monday and Tuesday 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. manoffmarketgardens.com
  • Roots to River Farm, New Hope. 3175 North Sugan Road, New Hope, PA 18938. The farm calls itself a "small diversified transitional organic farm" focused on heirloom varieties and year-round growing, and it offers CSA shares. Farm hours: Wednesday 3 to 7 p.m., Saturday noon to 5 p.m. rootstoriverfarm.com
  • Doylestown Farmers Market, Doylestown. South Hamilton Street between West Oakland Avenue and West State Street. Saturdays 8 a.m. to 1 p.m. through November 21, 2026. Vegetables, fruit, mushrooms, microgreens, meats, pasta and baked goods from local producers. Operated by the Bucks County Foodshed Alliance. doylestownfarmersmarket.bucksfoodshed.org
  • Wrightstown Farmers Market, Wrightstown area. Middletown Grange Fairgrounds, 576 Penns Park Road, Newtown, PA. A producer-only market with 35 to 40 farmers and makers each week, Saturdays 9 a.m. to noon through November 21, 2026. The market accepts SNAP EBT. wrightstownfarmersmarket.org
  • Milk House Farm Market, Newtown. 1118 Slack Road, Newtown, PA 18940. Pasture-raised eggs, produce, and what the farm describes as pasture-raised, organically fed chicken, turkey and pork. Open every day, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. milkhousefarmmarket.com

Lower Bucks

  • Shady Brook Farm Market, Yardley. 931 Stony Hill Road, Yardley, PA 19067. Produce grown on the farm, local honey, milk in glass bottles, eggs and cheeses. Sunday to Thursday 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., Friday and Saturday 9 a.m. to 10 p.m. shadybrookfarm.com
  • Charlann Farms, Yardley. 586 Stony Hill Road, Yardley, PA 19067. Sweet corn, tomatoes, peppers, squash, melons and pumpkins, plus items from local vendors. The fall festival began September 19. Hours aren't posted on the home page, so call ahead. charlannfarms.com
  • Styer Orchard, Langhorne. 97 Styers Lane, Langhorne, PA 19047. Pick-your-own apples and a pumpkin patch, open daily 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. in season, weather permitting. The farm store lists hours of Monday to Saturday 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. and Sunday 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. styerorchard.com
  • Snipes Farm and Education Center, Morrisville. 890 West Bridge Street, Morrisville, PA 19067. A nonprofit farm that states it is certified organic. Its market carries produce, plants, honey, pastured meats, eggs and dairy. The posted winter schedule is the second and fourth Saturdays, 10 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. Confirm fall hours with the farm. snipesfarm.org
