A free listing for every woman-run business in the county

Our directory is where Bucks County women go to find businesses run by other Bucks County women. A basic listing costs nothing. There's no trial period and no card to enter.

What a basic listing includes

  • Your business name and category
  • The town you're based in or the area you serve
  • A short description in your own words
  • Your phone number, website and Instagram handle
  • Your hours, if you keep regular ones

Anyone can browse the directory, whether or not they're a member, so your listing is visible to every reader.

Which businesses qualify

  • A woman runs it. The business is owned, co-owned or led day to day by a woman. Trans women are women, and their businesses are welcome here.
  • It's in Bucks County. You have a location here, or you're based here and serve customers here. Home-based businesses, solo practices and side businesses all count.
  • It's a real, operating business. We need a way for customers to reach you, such as a phone number, a website or an active social account.
  • It's legal and licensed where a license is required. If your field needs a license, such as health care, cosmetology, real estate or contracting, please hold a current one.

What we can't list

  • We can't list businesses outside Bucks County with no local base.
  • We don't list religious organizations, political campaigns or advocacy groups.
  • We leave out multi-level marketing recruitment and offers that require people to pay to join.
  • We won't list businesses that make health claims they can't support, or that turn customers away because of who they are.

How it works

Fill in the form below. We review each request by hand, usually within a week, and we may call or email to confirm a detail. You're telling us the business is woman-run, and we take you at your word unless something doesn't add up. Once your listing is live, you can ask us to update or remove it at any time through the contact page.

If you'd like more visibility, sponsored listings in the print edition are described on our advertising page. A sponsored listing is always optional, and it never affects whether your free listing is approved.
