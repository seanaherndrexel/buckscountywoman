A magazine that lives where you do

Bucks County Woman is an online and print magazine written for the women who live and work in Bucks County, Pennsylvania. We started it because the information women here need most is scattered across a dozen websites, a few Facebook groups and whatever a neighbor happens to remember. We're putting it in one place and keeping it current.

Some of that information is serious. You should be able to find a clinic, a crisis line or a safe place to go without making ten phone calls. Some of it is the good stuff: where people are shopping this month, which new business a woman down the road just opened, and where to meet other women who do what you do.

Who we write for

Every woman in Bucks County is welcome here. That means women of every age, race, ethnicity and background, women with disabilities, and LGBTQIA+ women, trans women included. If you live here, work here or are raising a family here, this magazine is yours.

We cover the whole county, from Quakertown and Perkasie down through Doylestown, New Hope and Newtown to Bensalem, Bristol and Levittown.

What you'll find in our pages

  • Women's health reporting. We report on local care in plain language: where the clinics and practices are, what they offer, and what has changed.
  • Resources and safe spaces. Our Resources & Safe Spaces page lists help lines and services we've checked ourselves.
  • Business events and meetups. We track networking groups, workshops and gatherings for women across the county.
  • Local finds. These are the shops, makers and places Bucks County women are talking about right now.
  • Women-run businesses. Our directory is free to join for women-run businesses in the county. You can add yours here.
  • A members-only community. Members get a private community app organized by town and by interest.

What we leave out

We don't publish religious content and we don't publish partisan politics. You won't find candidate endorsements, campaign coverage or faith-based advice in our pages or in our community app. Practical civic information, like a road closure or a meeting time, is fair game. There are plenty of other places to argue about the rest, and we'd like this to be the place where you can simply find what you need.

We're also strictly local. If it doesn't touch Bucks County, it doesn't run.

Ten percent goes back to local women

We give 10% of all proceeds to local organizations that serve women in Bucks County. That's 10% of everything the magazine takes in, including memberships, advertising and sponsorships. We're confirming our partner organizations now and we'll name them as soon as they've agreed. You can read exactly how the pledge works on our giving page.

Who publishes it

Bucks County Woman is published by Main Street Creative, an advertising and design agency on North Main Street in Doylestown. The legal entity behind the agency is Mini Machine Creative LLC.

The site launched in October 2026 as a demonstration edition, which means we're still building out sections and you may see some pages change. The first print issue is planned for Spring 2027. You can read about it on the print edition page.

Our articles are researched and drafted with AI assistance and reviewed by an editor before they're published. We explain how that works in our editorial standards.
