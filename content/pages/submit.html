You know this county better than any newsroom

A lot of what we publish starts with a reader. If you're hosting something, heard something or found something, send it our way. It's free to submit, and you don't need to be a member.

Events and meetups

We list events that happen in Bucks County and are open to women in the community. That includes business networking, workshops, health screenings, classes, fundraisers, book clubs, walking groups and casual meetups.

Please include the date and time, the venue and town, the cost, who is organizing it and a link or contact for questions. Accessibility details help a lot, so tell us if the space is step-free, if there's parking nearby and whether children are welcome.

Story tips

Tell us what we should be looking into. Useful tips include a clinic or service that opened, closed or changed, a woman doing remarkable work in your town, a new women-run business, or a gap in services that nobody is talking about. We'll keep your name private if you ask us to.

Local finds

Local Finds is where we share the shops, makers, meals and places that Bucks County women are excited about. If you've spotted something worth sharing, send us the name, the town and a line about what you liked.

When we feature an Instagram post in Local Finds, we use Instagram's official embed only, and we credit and tag the creator. We don't download or repost anyone's photos. If your post appears and you'd like it taken down, tell us and we'll remove it.

The ground rules

  • It has to be local. The event, business or story must be in Bucks County.
  • No religion and no partisan politics. We don't list worship services, faith-based programs, campaign events, rallies or candidate fundraisers.
  • It must be accurate. Double-check dates, addresses and prices before you send. We verify what we can, and we may contact you to confirm details.
  • It should welcome everyone. We don't list events that exclude women because of race, ethnicity, disability, sexual orientation or gender identity.
  • Health claims need care. We won't list events that promise cures or make medical claims that can't be supported.
  • Send only what's yours to share. Don't send photos or text you don't have the right to share, and don't send private details about other people.
  • Send it early. Two weeks' notice gives us time to check an event and get it in front of readers.

We review every submission and choose what runs. We can't promise that everything will be published, and we may edit for length and clarity. Paid promotion is handled separately on our advertising page, and businesses can claim a free directory listing at Get Listed.
