Every sale goes to the woman who made the work

A basic feature on this page costs the artist nothing, and Bucks County Woman takes no cut of what she sells. Each piece shown here carries a QR code. Scan it with your phone and you land on the artist's own shop, where you pay her directly.

The showcase is for women artists and makers who live or work in Bucks County, Pennsylvania. That includes painters, potters, jewelers, fiber artists, printmakers, woodworkers and anyone else making original work by hand.

From the page to her shop in one scan

  1. We feature the work. Photos of an artist's pieces run on this page and in the magazine, with her name, her town and a short description in her own words.
  2. Each feature gets a QR code. In the print edition the same code runs beside the artwork, so a reader holding the magazine can reach the shop as easily as a reader on the site.
  3. You scan and buy from her. The code opens the link the artist gave us, whether that's her own website, an online shop or a contact page for commissions.
  4. She keeps the sale. Payment, shipping and pickup are handled between you and the artist. We don't process orders and we don't take a percentage.

The door is open to every woman who makes things here

You can apply if you're a woman, you make original art or handmade goods, and you live or keep a studio in Bucks County. Trans women are women and are welcome here. So are women of every age, race, background and ability, from a teenager selling her first prints to a grandmother who has been throwing pots for forty years. You don't need gallery representation, a degree or a sales history.

Two things are required. The work has to be your own, and readers need a way to buy it from you.

Five things to send

  • Three to five photos of your work, clear and well lit. Phone photos are fine.
  • A short bio of a few sentences about you and what you make.
  • Your town in Bucks County.
  • Your medium, such as oil painting, stoneware, silver jewelry or weaving.
  • One link where people can buy your work. Your own website, an online shop or a page with your contact details all work.

There's no fee for the basic feature. We review every application and reply to each one. If your work is selected, we build your QR code from the link you send and show you the feature before it runs.

A note for readers

When you buy through a code on this page, you're buying from a neighbor. Questions about a piece, a custom order or a delivery go to the artist, and her contact details are on the shop the code opens. If a code or link ever fails, tell us through the contact page and we'll fix it.
