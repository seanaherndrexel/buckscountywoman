How we treat each other in the app

The Bucks County Woman community app is a private space for members. It works only if women feel safe asking a question, recommending a business or showing up to a meetup. These guidelines apply to every post, comment, event, listing and profile.

Be kind

Write to other members the way you'd speak to a neighbor you'll see again at the grocery store. You can disagree with an idea. You can't attack the person who shared it. Sarcasm at someone's expense, pile-ons and name-calling don't belong here.

Every woman belongs

This community is for women of every age, race, ethnicity and background, women with disabilities, and LGBTQIA+ women, trans women included. We don't allow hate, slurs, stereotypes or demeaning remarks about anyone's race, ethnicity, national origin, age, disability, body, sexual orientation, gender identity, income or family situation. Questioning whether another member belongs here counts as hate.

No harassment

  • Don't threaten, intimidate, stalk or repeatedly contact someone who has asked you to stop.
  • Don't follow a member from one group to another to argue with her.
  • Don't post sexual content or make unwanted advances.
  • Don't encourage others to target a member, a business owner or anyone else.

Politics and religion stay outside

We don't host debates about politics or religion. That means no campaigning, no posts about candidates or parties, no arguing over hot-button issues, and no promoting or criticizing any faith or lack of faith. Practical local information is welcome, such as a road closure, a school closing or the time of a township meeting. If a thread turns into a debate, a host will close it.

Protect privacy

  • Don't share another person's address, phone number, workplace, health details or photos without her permission.
  • Be careful with details about children, yours and other people's.
  • Don't screenshot posts and share them outside the app. What's said in a group stays in the group.
  • Some circles, such as those for LGBTQIA+ members, caregivers and members with disabilities, are especially private. Never reveal who belongs to them.
  • Think before posting anything that would tell a stranger where you live or when your home is empty.

No medical claims

Share your own experience freely, such as which practice you liked or what helped you sleep. Don't diagnose other members, tell anyone to stop a medication or a treatment, or promote products as cures. Posts that sell supplements or treatments with health promises will be removed. For anything medical, the right answer is a qualified clinician, and in an emergency it's 911.

Selling and self-promotion

The directory is the home for your business, and a basic listing is free. In your town group, you can mention your own business once a month. In the Women in Business circle, you can talk shop freely. Spam, recruiting for multi-level marketing and repeated sales posts aren't allowed.

Be honest

Use your real first name. Don't pretend to be someone else, and don't post information you know to be false. Recommendations should be real, so tell us if you have a stake in the business you're praising.

Reporting a problem

Every post, comment, event, listing and profile has a Report button. Use it when something breaks these guidelines. Reports are private, and the person you report isn't told who filed it. You can also block any member, which hides her posts from you and yours from her.

If someone is in danger, don't wait for a moderator. Call 911, or call or text 988 for a mental health crisis. Our Resources & Safe Spaces page lists local help lines.

How moderation works

  • Volunteer hosts in each group can hide a post and issue a warning. Only Bucks County Woman staff can suspend or remove a member.
  • Content reported by several members may be hidden automatically until a person reviews it.
  • We aim to review reports within 24 hours, and sooner when safety is involved.
  • Consequences usually go in steps: a warning, a seven-day pause on posting, a 30-day suspension, then removal. Serious violations such as threats or hate can lead to immediate removal.
  • You can appeal a decision once by email, and a staff member who wasn't involved in the original decision will answer it.

We moderate by these guidelines and nothing else. Hosts and staff follow them too.

Who can join

The app is for paying members who are 18 or older and who live or work in Bucks County. One person, one account.
