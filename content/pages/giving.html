Ten cents of every dollar stays with local women

Bucks County Woman gives 10% of all proceeds to local organizations that serve women in Bucks County. We made the pledge before we published a single article, and we wrote it down here so you can hold us to it.

How the 10% is figured

The pledge is 10% of all proceeds. That means 10% of every dollar the magazine takes in, counted before we pay our own expenses. It doesn't depend on whether we turn a profit.

The following all count toward the total:

  • Membership dues, both monthly and annual
  • Print edition add-on payments
  • Print and online advertising
  • Sponsored directory listings
  • Newsletter, section and event sponsorships
  • Ticket and table sales at our events

As an example, a $12 annual membership sends $1.20 to local women's organizations, and a $1,200 full-page ad sends $120.

Reported in public, every quarter

At the end of each calendar quarter we'll add up what the magazine took in, set aside 10% and publish the numbers on this page. Each report will show the total proceeds for the quarter, the amount given and where it went. If we ever miss a quarter, you'll see it here, and you should ask us about it.

The site launched in October 2026, so the first report will cover the final quarter of 2026.

Where the money will go

We're confirming our partner organizations now. We'll name them here once they've agreed, and not before, because it isn't fair to attach an organization's name to ours without its permission.

Here's what we look for in a partner:

  • It's a registered nonprofit that serves women in Bucks County.
  • Its work is local and its results can be seen here.
  • It serves every woman who needs it, regardless of race, ethnicity, age, disability, sexual orientation or gender identity.
  • It isn't a religious organization, a political party, a campaign or a lobbying group.

How an organization can apply

If you work or volunteer for a Bucks County organization that fits, we'd like to hear from you. Send a message through our contact page or email sean@mainstreetmakes.com with the following:

  • The organization's name, address and website
  • Its nonprofit status and EIN
  • A few sentences on who it serves in Bucks County and how
  • The best person for us to talk to

We review requests as they come in and revisit our list of partners once a year. Partner organizations don't pay anything and aren't asked to buy advertising. Being a partner also doesn't affect how we report on an organization, which is covered in our editorial standards.

How you can add to it

Every membership and every ad adds to the total. That's the whole idea: reading and supporting a local magazine should send money back into the county.
