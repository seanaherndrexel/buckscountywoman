Built so every woman can use it

A magazine for every woman in Bucks County has to work for women who use screen readers, who navigate by keyboard, who need larger text or who can't hear audio. Our goal is for the website and the members app to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

That's a goal we're working toward, and we don't claim to have reached it everywhere. The site launched in October 2026 and is still being built out, so some pages may fall short. When we find a problem, or you find one for us, we fix it.

What we're doing

  • We write in plain language, with clear headings in a logical order.
  • We add text descriptions to images that carry meaning.
  • We choose text and background colors with enough contrast to read comfortably.
  • We make sure pages work with a keyboard alone and show a visible focus indicator.
  • We label form fields and explain errors in words.
  • We design pages to reflow on small screens and when text is enlarged to 200%.
  • We avoid content that flashes, and we don't autoplay sound.
  • We publish magazine articles as real web pages, so you never need a PDF to read them.
  • We ask event organizers for accessibility details, such as step-free access, and include them in listings when we have them.

Where you may still hit a barrier

  • Embedded content from other companies. Instagram embeds, maps and the payment form are built by third parties, and we can't control everything about how they behave.
  • Reader submissions. Photos sent in by readers and members may arrive without descriptions. We add them where we can.
  • Print. The print edition is a paper magazine. Every print article will also be available on the site in an accessible format.

Tell us what isn't working

If something on the site or in the app is hard or impossible for you to use, we want to know. Please tell us:

  • The page address or the part of the app where it happened
  • What you were trying to do and what went wrong
  • The device, browser and any assistive technology you were using
