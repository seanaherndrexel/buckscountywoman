A magazine you can hold, coming Spring 2027

Bucks County Woman is going to print. The first issue is planned for Spring 2027, and we're aiming to have it out by the first day of spring. It'll be a full-color, standard-size glossy magazine filled with the same local reporting you read here, along with photography and a printed guide to women-run businesses.

Four issues a year to begin

We're starting with a quarterly schedule so that every issue is worth keeping on your coffee table:

  • Spring, in mid-March
  • Summer, in mid-June
  • Fall, in mid-September
  • Holiday, in mid-November

These dates are our plan, and we'll confirm each one here as it gets close. Between print issues, the digital magazine comes out every month.

Where to pick up a copy

The print edition will be free to pick up. We plan to print 5,000 copies of the first issue and place them where Bucks County women already spend time:

  • Salons, spas and studios
  • Coffee shops and cafes
  • Gyms and yoga, Pilates and barre studios
  • Women-run boutiques and gift shops
  • Medical and dental waiting rooms
  • Public libraries and community spaces
  • Local events and markets

We're signing up pickup locations now, and we'll publish the full list on this page before the first issue comes out. We're starting in central and upper Bucks and adding towns from there.

If you own or manage a place where women wait, gather or shop, we'd love to bring you a stack and a rack each quarter at no cost. Let us know and we'll add you to the route.

Home delivery for members

If you'd rather find it in your mailbox, members can add the print edition to their membership. The add-on is priced to cover printing and mailing, and we plan to offer it at $5 a month. It opens with the first print issue in Spring 2027. You can join now on the membership page and add print when it's available.

Ten percent of print proceeds goes to local women

Print is part of our giving pledge. Ten percent of print add-on payments and print advertising goes to local organizations serving women in Bucks County.

Put your business in the first issue

The first issue of a local magazine tends to get kept, passed around and talked about. If your customers are women in Bucks County, this is a good issue to be in. Introductory rates start at $275 for a business-card-size ad, and founding advertisers who book four issues save 20%. Sponsored listings in the printed women-owned business guide start at $150.

We plan to close ad reservations for the Spring 2027 issue on January 22, 2027. Sizes, rates and booking details are on the advertising page.

Have a story for print?

We're assigning stories for the first issue through the winter. If there's a woman, a business or a subject in your town that deserves a printed page, send us a tip.
