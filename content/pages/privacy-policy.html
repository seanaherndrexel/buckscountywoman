Effective date: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what information Bucks County Woman collects, why we collect it, who we share it with and what choices you have. Bucks County Woman is published by Main Street Creative, the business name of Mini Machine Creative LLC, 152 N Main St, Doylestown, PA 18901. In this policy, "we," "us" and "our" mean that company, and "the site" means this website and the members-only community app.

The short version is that we collect only what we need to run a local magazine and a members' community, we don't sell your personal data, and you can ask us to show you, correct or delete what we hold.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Email signups. When you sign up for the weekly email, we collect your email address and, if you provide them, your name and town.
  • Form submissions. When you use a form to contact us, submit an event, send a tip, or request a directory listing, we collect what you enter. That can include your name, email address, phone number, business details and the content of your message.
  • Membership accounts. When you become a member, we collect your name, email address, the town you choose as your home town, your login details, and your phone number if you provide it for account verification. Passwords are stored in a protected form that we can't read.
  • Member posts. In the community app, we store what you choose to share, including your profile, posts, comments, reactions, event responses, recommendations, photos you upload, business listings, reports you file and members you block.
  • Payment information. Payments will be handled by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe, and we never receive or store your full card number. We receive limited information back from Stripe, such as whether a payment succeeded, the type of card and its last four digits, the cardholder name, the billing ZIP code, and your membership plan and renewal date.

Information collected automatically

  • Cookies for login. We use cookies that are necessary to keep you signed in, keep your account secure and remember basic preferences. Details are in the cookies section below.
  • Basic technical information. Like most websites, our servers record information such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages requested and the date and time. We use this to keep the site running, measure which pages are read in aggregate and protect against abuse.
  • Email activity. Our email service may record whether an email was delivered and whether it was opened or a link was clicked, so we can keep our list healthy and see which stories readers find useful.

We don't collect your precise location. In the community app, we use only the town you select.

How we use your information

  • To send the weekly email and other emails you've asked for
  • To answer your messages and handle correction requests
  • To review and publish events, tips, local finds and directory listings
  • To create and manage your membership, process payments and send receipts and account notices
  • To run the community app, including showing your posts to other members and placing you in your town group
  • To confirm that members live or work in Bucks County, which may include checking your billing ZIP code
  • To moderate the community, enforce our community guidelines and terms of use, and respond to reports
  • To keep the site secure, prevent fraud and fix problems
  • To understand, in aggregate, what readers find useful so we can improve our coverage
  • To calculate and report our giving pledge, using totals only and never individual names
  • To comply with the law

What other members and the public can see

The community app is private to members. Other members can see your display name (your first name and last initial, unless you choose to show more), your home town, the date you joined, and anything you post. You decide whether your profile shows the interest circles you've joined, and membership in sensitive circles is hidden from other members by default.

Business directory listings and public events are visible to everyone, including people who aren't members, so include only details you're comfortable making public. Please remember that we can't prevent another member from copying or taking a screenshot of something you post, even though our guidelines forbid it.

We don't sell your personal data

We don't sell or rent your personal information to anyone, and we don't share it with others for their advertising. Advertisers and sponsors never receive our email list or member list. We may share overall figures with them, such as the number of subscribers, that can't be used to identify you.

Who we share information with

We share personal information only in these situations:

  • Service providers. Companies that help us operate, including Stripe for payments and providers of website hosting, data storage, email delivery and text message verification. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • When you ask us to publish something. If you submit an event or a directory listing, or agree to be named in a story, we publish the details intended for publication.
  • Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information when the law requires it, such as in response to a valid subpoena or court order, or when we believe it's necessary to protect someone from serious harm, to investigate fraud, or to protect our rights.
  • A change in ownership. If the magazine is sold or merged, your information may be transferred to the new owner, who must honor this policy or notify you of changes.

Cookies

We use cookies that are necessary for the site to work. These keep you logged in, protect your account and forms from misuse, and remember simple settings. You can block or delete cookies in your browser, but if you do, you won't be able to stay signed in to your membership.

We don't use advertising cookies, and we don't track you across other websites.

Some pages include content from other companies. Stripe sets its own cookies on payment pages to process payments and prevent fraud. Instagram posts shown in Local Finds use Instagram's official embed, which loads content from Instagram and may allow Instagram to set its own cookies and learn that you viewed the page. Embedded maps may work the same way. Those companies' own privacy policies govern what they collect.

How long we keep information

  • Email signups: until you unsubscribe. After that we keep a record of your address on a do-not-email list so we don't contact you again by mistake.
  • Form submissions: for as long as we need them to respond and to keep a record of what we published, generally up to three years.
  • Membership accounts and member posts: for as long as your account is open. If you delete your account, we remove your profile and posts within 30 days, though copies may remain in backups for a limited time.
  • Payment records: for as long as tax and accounting rules require, generally seven years.
  • Moderation records: for about 12 months, so we can apply our guidelines consistently.
  • Server logs: for a short period, generally no more than 90 days, unless needed to investigate a security problem.

Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link. Members will still receive essential account emails such as receipts.
  • See or correct your information. You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct anything that's wrong.
  • Delete your information. You can ask us to delete your account and personal information. We'll do so unless we have to keep something for legal, tax or safety reasons, and we'll tell you if that's the case.
  • Remove published content. You can ask us to update or remove your directory listing or event. If an Instagram post of yours is featured in Local Finds, you can ask us to remove it and we will.
  • Cancel your membership. You can cancel at any time.

To make a request, email sean@mainstreetmakes.com, call (215) 206-3657 or write to the address below. We'll respond within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we won't treat you differently for making a request. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's privacy law, and we'll honor valid requests made under those laws.

Sensitive topics

This magazine covers health and safety, and some readers contact us about private matters. Please share only what you're comfortable sharing. We don't ask for health information, and we don't use anything you tell us about your health, your safety, or your membership in a private circle for advertising. Tips sent in confidence are kept confidential, as described in our editorial standards.

Security

We protect your information with measures such as encrypted connections, restricted staff access and payment handling by Stripe. No website can promise perfect security. If a breach affects your personal information, we'll notify you as the law requires.

Children and teens

The site is intended for adults, and membership is limited to people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Where your information is stored

We're based in Pennsylvania, and our service providers store and process information in the United States.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the effective date. If a change is significant, we'll also tell members and email subscribers before it takes effect.
