How we keep ads and editorial apart

Advertisers don't get a say in what we report, and buying an ad doesn't buy coverage. Anything paid for is labeled as sponsored. This protects your ad as much as it protects our readers, because people trust the pages around it. We don't accept political or religious advertising. The full policy is in our editorial standards.

Ten percent of what you spend with us goes to local organizations serving women, as part of our giving pledge.
