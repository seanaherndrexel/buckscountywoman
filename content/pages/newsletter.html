Bucks County in your inbox, once a week

Our weekly email is free, and you don't need a membership to get it. It arrives once a week and takes about five minutes to read.

What's inside each week

  • What's happening. We pick out business events, meetups, classes and gatherings for women around the county in the week ahead.
  • New in the magazine. You'll get our latest articles, including women's health reporting and guides to local services.
  • Local finds. We share the shops, makers and places Bucks County women are talking about.
  • A woman-run business to know. Each week we introduce one business from our directory.
  • Useful updates. We pass along changes to local services and resources that are worth knowing about.

You won't find religion or partisan politics in it, and everything is local to Bucks County.

What we do with your email address

We use it to send you this email, and that's all. We don't sell or rent our list. Every email has an unsubscribe link that works with one click. Some issues may carry a sponsor, and when they do, the sponsor's message is clearly labeled and the sponsor never receives your address. The details are in our privacy policy.
