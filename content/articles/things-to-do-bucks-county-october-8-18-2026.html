Four of the best things on the county calendar between now and Sunday, October 18 cost nothing to walk into. Two of them land this Saturday, and the other two arrive the Saturday after.

We checked every date, hour and price below against the organizer's own page this week. Where a venue doesn't publish a detail, we say so instead of guessing. Hours shift with weather in October, so glance at the link before you leave the house.

This weekend, October 10 and 11

A village green in Fallsington

Historic Fallsington Day is Saturday, October 10. Admission is free. The preserved village fills with crafters, vendors, food and entertainment for the day, centered on the visitors center at 4 Yardley Avenue. Park for free at Fallsington Elementary School and walk down Yardley Avenue. The organizer doesn't post start and end times, so call ahead if you're timing a nap schedule.

Brass bands among the scarecrows

Peddler's Village in Lahaska holds OctoberFeast on Saturday and Sunday, October 10 and 11, from 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. Admission and parking are free. Expect brass bands, a beer garden, food trucks and a petting zoo from noon to 4 p.m. both days. Scarecrow making, pumpkin painting and axe throwing are pay-as-you-go. Shops stay open until 8 p.m. on Saturday. The village address is 2400 Street Road, New Hope.

The Scarecrows in the Village display is free to walk every day and continues into the last week of October. It's a good solo hour on a weekday morning, when the brick paths are quiet.

Fresh juice at the wineries

The Bucks County Wine Trail holds its Harvest Celebration the same two days. Seven members take part, from Aviator and Bishop Estate in Perkasie to Crossing Vineyards in Washington Crossing and Rose Bank in Newtown. Each one sets its own schedule and pricing, and the trail asks visitors to call ahead. Pick two that sit close together and name a driver.

A free Sunday at the Michener

The Michener Art Museum, 138 South Pine Street in Doylestown, waives admission on the second Sunday of each month. That's October 11, noon to 5 p.m., special exhibitions included. A free family workshop on spider web weaving runs 1 to 3 p.m., and the museum recommends registering early. Regular admission is $15 for adults, $13 for seniors and $5 for ages 6 to 18.

Stars over Lake Nockamixon

Upper Bucks gets the darkest sky. Nockamixon State Park lists Starwatch for Saturday, October 10, from 7 to 10 p.m. at the Tohickon Boat Launch in Ottsville. It's free and needs no registration. Dress for standing still in the cold.

Castle hours in Doylestown

October suits Henry Mercer's concrete castles. Mercer Museum and Fonthill Castle are both open Tuesday to Sunday, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., at $20 for adults, $17 for seniors, $15 for students and $10 for ages 6 to 17.

Fonthill, at 525 East Court Street, opens for self-paced Meander Days on Friday, October 9 and Saturday, October 10, from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. at regular admission. On Friday, a talk on Gothic literature and the castle runs at noon and again at 3 p.m. for $30. Sunday evening brings yoga on the front lawn from 5:30 to 6:30 p.m. for $20.

Read the access notes first. The museum says Fonthill has steep stairs and narrow, uneven passages and doesn't recommend it for visitors with limited mobility. At the Mercer Museum, 84 South Pine Street, the modern ground-floor wing is fully accessible, and the historic castle has an elevator with partial access.

Midweek, when the crowds thin

Nockamixon runs a free Fall Foliage Pontoon Boat Tour on Thursday, October 15, with sessions from 1 to 3 p.m. and 4 to 6 p.m. Boats leave from the Marina Visitor Center at 1 Harrisburg School Road. Registration is required through the state's events page, and the park office answers questions at 215-529-7300.

Bowman's Hill Wildflower Preserve, 1635 River Road in New Hope, is open 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. every day except Tuesday, with last admission at 4 p.m. Adults pay $12, seniors, students and veterans $9, and children 5 to 17 pay $6. Visitors with ACCESS, SNAP or EBT cards pay $2 for up to four people at the desk. The preserve also runs a reserved Access Cart tour for visitors with limited mobility. Book it at least 48 hours ahead at 215-862-2924, extension 102. The cart can't carry a fixed wheelchair.

Down the road, the grounds of Washington Crossing Historic Park are free from dawn to dusk. The visitor center at 1112 River Road is open daily from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. but closes on Monday, October 12 for Indigenous Peoples' Day.

For a drive, Visit Bucks County maps a self-guided covered bridge tour of the county's 12 remaining bridges. The western loop covers about 38 miles in roughly an hour and a half, starting at Moods Covered Bridge in Perkasie. The eastern loop is longer, at about 58 miles.

Two curtains, one river

Bristol Riverside Theatre, 120 Radcliffe Street in Bristol, is staging "Dracula: A Comedy of Terrors" through October 25. It runs without an intermission. Parking is free, the theatre is wheelchair accessible and assisted listening devices are available. The box office is 215-785-0100.

Upriver, "Rock of Ages" opens Saturday, October 10 at Bucks County Playhouse, 70 South Main Street in New Hope, and plays through November 1. The Playhouse lists wheelchair spaces and assistive listening devices, plus an ASL-interpreted performance on Sunday, October 25 at 1:30 p.m. Neither theater posts a single ticket price, so check the seat map for your date.

In Upper Bucks, Sellersville Theater at 24 West Temple Avenue has Acoustic Alchemy on Friday, October 16 at 8 p.m. and the John Pizzarelli Trio on Sunday, October 18.

Saturday, October 17: Broad Street and the riverfront

Autumn Alive closes Broad Street in downtown Quakertown from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. The draw is the pet parade, with animals in Halloween costumes. There's also live music, a children's courtyard, a food court, an authors' corner and a beer garden for guests 21 and older. The rain date is October 24.

The same day, Bristol Borough marks the 50th Historic Bristol Day from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. The Bristol Cultural and Historical Foundation lists a classic car show, live entertainment, artisan vendors, a petting zoo and children's activities. Tours of private historic homes and a tea require a ticket, sold through the foundation's site.

For the pumpkin-and-hayride crowd

Shady Brook Farm, 931 Stony Hill Road in Yardley, runs FallFest through October 30. Tickets are priced by date. Most weekdays are listed at $20, and Sundays reach $35. The after-dark Eerie Illuminations light show is closed Thursday, October 8 and Monday, October 12. Pets and outside food aren't allowed. The farm doesn't post gate hours on its ticket page, so confirm when you buy.

Quick answers

What's free in Bucks County this week?

Historic Fallsington Day, OctoberFeast and the scarecrow display at Peddler's Village, the Michener on Sunday, October 11, both Nockamixon programs and the grounds at Washington Crossing all cost nothing. Autumn Alive in Quakertown lists no admission fee.

Which outings work well alone?

The Michener, a Fonthill Meander Day, a weekday walk at Bowman's Hill and a matinee in Bristol are all easy to do solo. The pontoon tour takes individual registrations too.

Which venues publish accessibility details?

Bristol Riverside Theatre, Bucks County Playhouse, Bowman's Hill and the Mercer Museum all do. Fonthill Castle is candid that its stairs and passages limit access. For the street festivals, call the organizer with specific questions.

Do I need tickets in advance?

Register ahead for the Nockamixon boat tour and the Michener family workshop. Buy Shady Brook, theater and Bristol house tour tickets online before you go. The street festivals are walk-up.
