Friday, October 16 has two events on it, in two different counties, and they overlap. At 11:30 a.m. the Bucks County Business Women's Alliance sits down to lunch in New Hope. At 1 p.m. the Philadelphia chapter of the National Association of Women Business Owners opens its first pitch competition at Villanova University, with $40,000 in prize money on the table.

We checked every listing below against the organizer's own calendar this week. Where an organizer hasn't posted a price or a room, we say so and leave the blank for you to fill with a phone call. One correction to our own calendar is in here too.

Before the weekend

Friday, October 9, 9 to 10 a.m. NAWBO Philadelphia's "Power Friday and Beyond" series takes up a problem most owners know well: how to take real time off when the business depends on you. The chapter calendar doesn't list a venue or a price for this one, so register through the listing to get the details.

Friday, October 9, 8 a.m. The Central Bucks Chamber of Commerce holds its State of the State Address. The chamber's homepage lists the date and start time; the venue and ticket price sit behind the registration link there.

Midweek, and a date to fix in your phone

Wednesday, October 14, 9:30 to 11:30 a.m. Last week our calendar listed NAWBO's in-person session in Wayne as a "Power Friday." The name is right and the date is right, but October 14 is a Wednesday. The session, "Integrating Work and Life in Ways that Make Sense for YOU," is led by Lesley Mallow Wendell and meets at Entrust Financial, 487 Devon Park Drive, Suite 216, Wayne. NAWBO's listing doesn't post a price.

Wednesday, October 14, 11 a.m. The Lower Bucks County Chamber of Commerce holds its Women's Leadership Event. The chamber's site confirms the date and the 11 a.m. start. We couldn't load the registration page to confirm the venue or ticket price, so call the chamber at 215-943-7400 before you plan around it. The chamber's Women in Business Committee, which meets at 9 a.m. on the first Wednesday of each month, is the group behind this kind of programming.

Wednesday, October 14, 6 to 8 p.m. If a credential is part of your next move, Bucks County Community College opens its Bristol campus for an open house at 1304 Veterans Highway. A culinary and hospitality open house follows on Wednesday, October 21, from 6 to 7:30 p.m. in Founders Hall 244 on the Newtown campus, 275 Swamp Road.

Thursday in Quakertown or Ambler

Thursday, October 15, 9 to 10 a.m. The Upper Bucks Chamber of Commerce runs a "Coffee & Convos" session on geofencing and local advertising at its office, 21 N. Main Street, Quakertown. The event listing carries both a "members only" and a "public" tag, which is not much help. Call 215-536-3211 to ask whether a guest seat is open.

Thursday, October 15, 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. Thrive HER, billed as a NAWBO women's health experience, sets up at Temple University's Ambler campus, 580 Meetinghouse Road. It's an expo format with vendor tables. The event's ticket listing shows admission at $5 for NAWBO and alliance members and $10 for everyone else, plus Eventbrite fees, with no refunds. Vendor tables run $50 for members and $100 for non-members.

The Friday you have to choose

New Hope, 11:30 a.m.

The Bucks County Business Women's Alliance holds its monthly lunch on Friday, October 16, from 11:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. at Jericho National Golf Club, 250 Brownsburg Road East, New Hope. The topic is "AI Workflows for Business." The alliance describes it as a plain-language method for writing better prompts, then turning the ones that work into repeatable routines for marketing, sales, client messages and operations. No technical background is assumed.

Guests are welcome. Tickets cost $37 for members and $42 for non-members, and $42 for anyone paying at the door.

Villanova, 1 p.m.

She Pitches Philly runs from 1 to 4 p.m. on Friday, October 16 in the Villanova Room of the Connelly Center at Villanova University, 800 E. Lancaster Avenue. NAWBO Philadelphia says early-stage and small growth-stage women-owned businesses will compete for $40,000 in total prizes. Applications to pitch closed on September 15, so this is now a spectator event, and a useful one if you plan to ask for money yourself next year. Audience tickets are sold through the competition's website. We couldn't confirm the ticket price before publication.

Also that evening, the Greater BucksMont Chamber of Commerce lists a fundraising handbag bingo run by its Women2Women group at 5:30 p.m., and a Women2Women meeting at 9 a.m. on Thursday, October 22. The chamber's homepage gives dates and times only. Contact the chamber for the location and guest policy.

Quick answers

Do I have to be a member to attend these events?

Not for most of them. The Business Women's Alliance lunch, Thrive HER and the SCORE webinars all sell or offer seats to non-members, usually for a few dollars more. Chamber events vary, and a few listings this month don't say, so a quick call to the chamber office settles it.

What does a typical event cost a non-member?

This fortnight the posted prices run from free (several SCORE webinars) to $10 (Thrive HER, and SCORE's evening sessions) to $42 (the Business Women's Alliance lunch, meal included).

Is anything happening in the evening for women who can't leave work midday?

SCORE's 6 p.m. online workshops on October 15, 19 and 21 are built for that. The Bucks County Community College open houses on October 14 and 21 also start at 6 p.m.

Can I still enter She Pitches Philly?

No. The application deadline was September 15. You can attend as an audience member on October 16, and Spark Bowl on November 19 is the next local pitch event to watch.
