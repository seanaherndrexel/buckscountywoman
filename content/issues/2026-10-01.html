Bucks County has 646,000 residents and, until this week, no magazine written for the women who hold it together. This is the first issue of the one we wanted to read.

We started with the questions that take an afternoon of phone calls to answer. Which hospital still delivers babies in Upper Bucks. Where a woman without insurance can get a mammogram. Which business groups are meeting between now and December. Every address and phone number in these pages was checked on the organization's own website before we printed it.

A new issue arrives every Thursday. Tell us what you want in the next one.
