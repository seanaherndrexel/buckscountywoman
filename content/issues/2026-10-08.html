A study published in September re-read all eight of the randomized mammography trials and came back with a number under 5 percent for overdiagnosis, far below the 30 to 50 percent figures that have circulated for years. Our health story this week explains what that does and doesn't change, and where to book a screening in Bucks County.

October is also when the county's support organizations ask for attention, so this issue carries a guide to the hotlines, court offices and housing lines a woman can call for safety. Where an organization's own website was unavailable to us this week, we relied on Bucks County government listings and said so in the piece.

The rest is the working calendar of a fall week: business events through October 22, places to show up alone and be welcome, eleven days of things to do, fourteen independent shops, free advice and loan programs for founders, the week's recalls, and a Halloween planner for a holiday that lands on a Saturday. We close at Green Hills Farm in Hilltown Township with Pearl S. Buck, quoted only from her published speeches.

We left out every event whose date, place or price we couldn't confirm. A new issue arrives next Thursday.
