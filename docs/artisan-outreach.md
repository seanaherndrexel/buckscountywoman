## Invitation email template

Send one at a time, from Sean, after confirming the open items above. Replace the bracketed parts.

**Subject:** Featuring your work in Bucks County Woman

Hi [First name],

I'm Sean Ahern, publisher of Bucks County Woman, a new online and print magazine for women in Bucks County. I came across your [medium] at [her website] and I'd like to feature it.

We're building a Local Artisans showcase for women artists and makers in the county. Your work would run on our site and in the magazine with a QR code beside it. A reader scans the code and lands on your shop, and she buys from you directly. You keep the whole sale. There's no fee for the feature and we take no percentage.

To get started I'd need four things from you:

- Three to five photos of your work
- A short bio, a few sentences is plenty
- Your town
- The link where people can buy from you

You can reply to this email with those, or use the form at [site URL]/artisans. I'll send you the feature to approve before anything is published.

If it isn't a fit, no problem at all, and thank you for the work you're putting into this county.

Sean Ahern
