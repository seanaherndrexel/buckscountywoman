## Task C: Print add-on pricing

### Assumptions

- 32 pages, 8.375 x 10.875, full color, saddle-stitched, mailed as a tabbed self-mailer with an address panel (no envelope, no poly bag).
- Weight is roughly 4.5 to 5 oz on 70# text / 80# cover. That puts it **just over the 4 oz line**, so Marketing Mail charges it by the pound. A lighter 60# text sheet could keep it under 4 oz.
- Monthly runs of 500, 1,000 or 2,000 subscriber copies.
- 10% of gross goes to charity, so it comes off the top of every dollar.

### Print cost per copy (published 2026 price examples)

|---|---|---|---|

| Design One Printing guide | 40 pp | 1,000 | $0.71 |
| QinPrinting (offset, overseas, gloss, laminated cover) | 32+4 pp, 8.5x11 | 500 / 1,000 / 2,000 | $1.41 / $1.79 / $2.67 (their chart; likely total-run tiers) |

| MagCloud (print on demand) | $0.17 to $0.36 per page | 100+ | about $5 to $8 for 32 pp |

The sources disagree, so I used conservative US digital short-run figures: **$2.25 at 500, $1.60 at 1,000, $1.20 at 2,000.** Get real quotes from Mixam, Greenerprinter and a local Bucks printer before launch; those calculators would not load for automated reading.

### Postage per piece (USPS Notice 123, effective July 12, 2026)

| Class | Price | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing Mail commercial flats, 4 oz or less | Auto 5-Digit $0.783, Auto 3-Digit $1.021, Mixed $1.260 | Over 4 oz adds a per-pound charge ($0.797/lb, origin entry) |
| Periodicals Outside-County | Piece: 5-Digit $0.746, 3-Digit $0.905, Mixed $1.053, Carrier Route $0.474; Pound: $0.300 ad / $0.205 editorial (origin) | Needs USPS Periodicals authorization (Form 3500, about $375), a paid list, and a regular schedule |
| **Periodicals In-County** | Piece: Basic $0.153, High Density $0.109; Pound: $0.204 non-DDU | Applies to copies mailed to addresses in the county of publication. For Bucks subscribers this is about **$0.22 a copy** |
| First-Class commercial flat, 5 oz | $2.185 | Too expensive; skip |

A 5 oz copy by Marketing Mail lands around **$0.80 to $0.85**. If Bucks County Woman qualifies for Periodicals, most copies go to Bucks addresses at the In-County rate, around **$0.22**. That is the single biggest saving available and worth applying for once there is a paid list.

Mail-house prep (CASS/NCOA, inkjet address, tab, presort): about $0.07 a piece per Mail Processing Associates; I used $0.10. Packaging/spoilage allowance: $0.03 to $0.10.

### Cost per copy and break-even price

Price needed = (cost + $0.30 Stripe fixed) / (1 minus 2.9% Stripe minus 10% charity) = (cost + 0.30) / 0.871

| Monthly run | Print | Postage (Marketing Mail) | Mail prep | Spoilage | **Cost/copy** | **Break-even monthly charge** | Margin at $5/mo |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 500 | $2.25 | $0.85 | $0.10 | $0.10 | **$3.30** | **$4.13** | $0.76 |
| 1,000 | $1.60 | $0.85 | $0.10 | $0.05 | **$2.60** | **$3.33** | $1.46 |
| 2,000 | $1.20 | $0.80 | $0.10 | $0.03 | **$2.13** | **$2.79** | $1.93 |

Net from a $5 charge = $5 minus $0.445 Stripe minus $0.50 charity = $4.06.

### Recommendation

- **Print add-on: $5/month**, on top of the $1 digital membership ($6/month total). It covers cost at 500 copies with about a 76 cent cushion and gets healthier as the list grows. If Periodicals In-County is approved, the cushion grows by about $0.60 a copy.
- **Better: sell print annually at $59/year** (or $69/year bundled with digital). Stripe on $59 is $2.01 (3.4%) versus $5.34 across twelve $5 charges, and it locks in a year of copies for printer planning. At 500 copies, a year costs $39.60 to produce and mail; $59 minus $2.01 Stripe minus $5.90 charity leaves $51.09, a cushion of about $11.50 per subscriber.
- Do not go below $4/month for print until the run passes 1,000 copies.

### The Stripe bite on $1

| Plan | Gross per year | Stripe fees | Charity 10% | **Net per year** | Stripe as % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $1/month | $12.00 | 12 x $0.329 = $3.95 | $1.20 | **$6.85** | 32.9% |
| $12/year | $12.00 | $0.648 | $1.20 | **$10.15** | 5.4% |

Each $1 monthly charge loses 33 cents to Stripe before the charity share. The annual plan keeps $3.30 more per member per year, about 48% more money. Card declines and expirations on monthly billing also hit 12 times a year instead of once.

**Recommendation: offer both, but lead with $12/year.** Keep "$1 a month" as the headline price because it sells, and show "or $12 a year" as the default selected option. Monthly members cost 33 cents a charge in fees, so keep it available as the low-commitment door, not the main path.
