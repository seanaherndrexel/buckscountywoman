# Bucks County Woman: Competitive and Model Research

Compiled October 1, 2026. Sources are linked inline. Anything marked **[UNVERIFIED]** could not be confirmed from a primary source during this research.

---

## 1. Existing women-focused publications serving Bucks County and the Philadelphia suburbs

### Bucks County Women's Journal (the direct incumbent)
- **URL:** https://buckscountywomensjournal.com/ (older article archive at https://bcwomensjournal.com/)
- **Format:** Free print tabloid ("Take One, It's Priceless!") with PDF issues online. Tagline: "the only educational newspaper serving the women of Bucks County."
- **Frequency:** Six times a year, bimonthly (e.g. Feb/Mar 2025, Apr/May 2025, Oct/Nov 2025 issues).
- **Founded:** June 2006.
- **Readership claim:** 40,000 readers (self-reported; print run not stated).
- **Audience claim:** Women 25-65, upper-income, higher-educated, living or working in Bucks County.
- **Distribution:** Pick-up racks at salons, gyms, coffee shops, medical offices, libraries, banks, specialty food shops.
- **Sections (from current issue):** Photography, Health & Beauty, Life Coaching, Personal Growth & Spirituality, Community, Arts & Leisure, Art Gallery, Food & Wine, Business Profiles, Real Estate, Professional Services, Health & Medicine, Charitable Work, Wealth Management, Business columns. Recurring Profile (business leader), wine column, events calendar.
- **Website nav:** Home, Issues, Advertising Information, Testimonials, Partners, Contact Us.
- **Revenue:** Advertising, and specifically paid advertorial. The publisher states "editorials are more effective than placement ads"; most articles are written by advertisers (landscapers, maid services, wellness practitioners). Rates not published.
- **Publisher:** Robin Coppinger. Mailing address is a PO box in Albrightsville, PA.
- **Gaps / weaknesses observed:** Thin website with no real digital editorial, no newsletter found, no events, no directory, no awards franchise, content is mostly advertiser-written. Includes spirituality content (Bucks County Woman's no-religion stance is a clear differentiator).
- Sources: [home](https://buckscountywomensjournal.com/), [advertising info](https://buckscountywomensjournal.com/advertising-information/), [Feb/Mar 2025 PDF](https://buckscountywomensjournal.com/wp-content/uploads/FebMar25_Full.pdf)

### Bucks County Parent: Women of Influence Guide
- **URL:** https://buckscountyparent.com/women/
- **Format:** Annual guide, print and digital; directory of women-owned/women-led businesses and nonprofits, plus awards and a luncheon. In its 4th year (2026 winners pending).
- **Revenue:** Pay-to-play. Inclusion requires buying a Women of Influence Luncheon ticket or a sponsorship, which secures a listing in print and digital.
- **Publisher:** Family Focus Media, LLC. Distributed via Bucks County Parent print, website, newsletters, Facebook groups.
- **Takeaway:** Proves local women business owners will pay for directory listing bundled with an event ticket.

### Bucks Happening magazine: "Happening Women"
- **URL:** https://bucks.happeningmag.com/happening-women-2022/
- General-interest Bucks lifestyle magazine with an annual reader-nominated "Happening Women" feature (2022: at least 9 honorees, photo shoots at the Michener Museum and Newtown Theatre, sponsors including Jefferson Outpatient Imaging, LaChele Medical Aesthetics, Bucks County Community College). Not women-only. Whether it continued after 2022 is **[UNVERIFIED]**.

### The Women's Journal (Delaware & Chester County PA editions, plus Delaware state)
- **URL:** https://thewomensjournal.com/
- **Format:** Free quarterly print newspaper; separate editions for Delaware & Chester County (PA), New Castle and Kent County (DE).
- **Readership claim:** 80,000+ combined across two papers (uses NNA's 3.46 readers-per-copy multiplier, so actual print run is far lower).
- **Sections:** Bridal, Business & Finance, Family, Events, Fashion & Beauty, Food & Wine, Health & Medicine, Home & Garden, Legal, Travel, Women in Profile.
- **Distribution:** Health clubs, retail, spas, salons, physician offices, restaurants.
- **Revenue:** Advertising (pitch: women influence 80%+ of purchases). Part of the "County Woman" newspaper model (claims 50 years, 370+ publications nationally; **[UNVERIFIED]** beyond their own page). Publisher: Pamela Rizzo.

### Montgomery County Women's Journal / Lehigh Valley Woman
- Co-publisher Celeste Kline per [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/celeste-kline-a4032520/); Lehigh Valley Woman has a [Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/p/Lehigh-Valley-Woman-100071882550274/). No working website, frequency or circulation found. **[UNVERIFIED]** whether still publishing.

- **Lehigh Valley Style:** annual "Influential Women of the Year" (6 honorees, August issue). https://lehighvalleystyle.com/people/interesting-people/2024-influential-women-of-the-year/ Also runs women's social-group roundups: https://lehighvalleystyle.com/everything-lv/around-town/womens-social-groups/

### Women's business organizations in Bucks (partners / event calendar sources, not publications)
- **Women's Business Forum of Bucks County:** closed July 2019 after 15 years of free monthly Doylestown meetings ([source](https://montgomerycountyalive.com/bloginfo/the-womens-business-forum-of-bucks-county-is-hosting-its-final-meeting-with-a-mission-accomplished-celebration.cfm)). The gap it left is an opening.
- **Bucks County Business Women's Alliance:** monthly lunches 3rd Friday at Jericho National Golf Club, New Hope; Newtown-based nonprofit. https://www.buckscountybwa.com/
- **Central Bucks Chamber Women in Business Committee:** monthly meetings, March Leadership Summit; on hiatus fall 2026, reconvening January 2027. https://centralbuckschamber.com/women-in-business-committee/
- **Bucks County Women in Business Summit:** organized by Sarah Peters; 3rd annual on Oct 9, 2025 at Black Bass Hotel, ~75 attendees, proceeds to Kin Wellness & Support Center, sip-and-shop at Peddler's Village. https://buckscountyparent.com/third-annual-bucks-county-women-in-business-summit/

**Market read:** The only dedicated women's title in Bucks is a 20-year-old advertorial tabloid with almost no digital, events or community layer. Nobody owns the combination of real editorial, a women-run business directory, an events calendar, and a signature awards franchise for Bucks County.

## 3. Model to emulate: BRAVA Magazine

BRAVA is the closest structural match to a Doylestown-based launch: a single-county, bimonthly, glossy women's title owned by a regional media/agency group, which is exactly what Main Street Creative can be for Bucks County Woman. It has the most fully published, proven business model of any title found: a transparent rate card, paid editorial products (Professional Profiles, Ask the Expert, Shop Local) that local businesses can afford, a $575/month digital partnership, and resold digital services that map directly onto an agency's existing offerings. Its revenue is spread across four sponsorable annual events (Women to Watch, Raves & Faves, Women in Business, a night-out social) instead of depending on display ads alone. Borrow two additions from the others: Minnesota Women's Press's annual women-led business directory and inclusive voice, and Today's Woman's community-sisterhood energy (networking socials, merch, "We See You, Sis!" tone).

## 4. Recommended 10 categories for Bucks County Woman

1. **Health & Wellness** — Clear, current reporting on women's health, from where to find care in Bucks County to what new treatments and research mean for you, told through real local stories.
2. **Care Near You (Clinic & Provider Finder)** — A map-and-list guide to OB-GYNs, midwives, mammography, menopause, fertility, mental health and other women's care locations across the county.
3. **Resources & Safe Spaces** — Support services, hotlines, shelters, LGBTQIA+-affirming spaces, and accessible programs, gathered in one place so no one has to search alone.
4. **Business & Career** — Profiles, advice and news for women who run businesses, lead teams, or are figuring out their next move.
5. **Business Events** — Every networking lunch, workshop, summit and pitch night for Bucks County women in business, in one calendar.
6. **Meetups & Community** — Book clubs, running groups, new-mom circles, hobby nights and every other way to find your people here.
7. **Out & About (Local Events)** — Weekend picks, festivals, arts, classes and family outings from Yardley to Quakertown.
8. **Local Finds (Shopping)** — The shops, makers and boutiques worth the drive, with an eye on women-owned spots on every Main Street in the county.
9. **Women-Run Business Directory** — A searchable guide to businesses owned and led by Bucks County women, sorted by what you need.
10. **Her Story (People & Profiles)** — The women of every age and background who make this county what it is, in their own words.

## 5. Ten recurring franchise / feature ideas

1. **Women to Watch** (annual, January issue + event) — BRAVA's longest-running franchise since 2012; reader nominations, 10-12 honorees with plans for the year ahead.
2. **Most Admired / Bucks County Woman Awards gala** — Today's Woman model: open nominations, categories (Young Leaders, Nonprofit, Public Service, Arts, Health), finalists then winners; sponsored luncheon/gala.
3. **Annual Women-Run Business Directory issue** — Minnesota Women's Press 24-page September insert; online version year-round; paid enhanced listings (Bucks County Parent proves local willingness to pay).
4. **Reader's Choice ("Bucks Raves")** — BRAVA's Raves & Faves: annual reader poll of favorite local businesses, winners get decals and a celebration event.
5. **Women in Business issue + celebration** — BRAVA Sep/Oct issue tied to an event; fills the gap left by the Women's Business Forum of Bucks County (closed 2019).
6. **Ask the Expert** — Paid expert Q&A column (doctors, financial planners, attorneys) with clear sponsored labeling; BRAVA prices $825-1,200.
7. **Shop Local page** — BRAVA's small-format ($450) shopping placement grouped into one editorial-looking page; ideal entry point for boutiques.
8. **Monthly networking social ("BCW Night Out")** — Today's Woman's WSYS socials and BRAVA Night Out; rotate host venues among women-owned businesses (Lehigh Valley "Girls Pint Out" model).
9. **First-person "Perspective" essays** — Minnesota Women's Press format; reader-submitted personal stories, rotating voices across ages, abilities, cultures and identities.
10. **Health case study series** — A recurring feature pairing a local patient story with a local clinician on a current women's health advance (menopause care, breast imaging, pelvic health, fertility), anchored to the Care Near You finder.

Supporting newsletter plan drawn from the models: a weekly newsletter (Today's Woman's "The T" / BRAVA Friday Recap), plus a monthly business-women newsletter (BRAVA Business Monthly: small list, 58% open rate, sellable sponsorship).
